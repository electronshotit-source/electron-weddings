Behind The Lens

Hi, I'm behind the lens at Electron Weddings.

I photograph weddings the way they actually feel — a little chaotic, deeply tender, and over far too fast. My approach is documentary first: I stay close, stay quiet, and let the day unfold in front of the camera rather than direct it.

Whether it's an intimate elopement in the mountains or a three-day celebration surrounded by everyone you love, my goal is the same — to hand you photographs that still make you cry happy tears twenty years from now.
